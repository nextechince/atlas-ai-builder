Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what you want and edit by chatting. The code stays hidden.

Will an edit break my site?

Atlas changes only what you ask for, and you can undo any change.

How do I publish?

Open a project, press Publish, choose a name and your site goes live on your own link.

Can I build in my own language?

Yes. Pick a language next to the model picker and the site and chat follow it.

Are my projects saved?

Yes. They save on your device and sync to your account when you are logged in.
